- F-S5-02: the gate's "stats == recount" can't use `scripts/drive/recount.ts`'s full replay at today's size. Started
  10:28 local against ops `/index/recount`, it walked the engine's logs from block 37,370,025 and after an hour (its
  background limit) was near 37,425,000 of ~38,971,000: Moderato refused most windows ("query exceeds max results

  blocks an hour a full replay takes more than a day. Open: the recount needs a cheaper chain side — the hourly
  `indexer-recount` actor's Tempo Indexer API counts per event (C.4.3; idle without `TEMPO_INDEXER_API_KEY`), or a
  per-range replay that recounts recent ranges against `countsThrough`.
