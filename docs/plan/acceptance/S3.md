## F-S3-08 — the settler starved (found 2026-10-09 at the S23 gate)

From roughly noon UTC on 2026-10-09 the settler sent nothing ("300 Windows tracked · 200 due · nothing sent yet")
while 500+ expired Windows had both prints in and stayed unresolved (counted through `/api/index/markets` and the
lens at 20:20Z: expiries 18:35–20:15Z all unresolved; every expiry before 12:00Z resolved). Found because the S8 vault

was re-added from the index next pass, filling the 200 due slots ahead of every Window that needed settling. Fixed in
the settler commit after 8837084 (open Windows first, done Windows remembered for the process, past waits re-checked
on the 10 s print poll); the live result is recorded below once ops runs it. Parity: M-05 (settlement, redemption,
void).
