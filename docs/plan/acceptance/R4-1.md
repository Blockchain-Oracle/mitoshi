# R4-1 acceptance evidence — positions P&L (D-227)

The rule: a position's P&L is what Close pays now (the bid walk) — or the known payout once its Window has its answer,
or its open cost while locked or unpriced — less the **open** cost of what is held; a sale lowers the open cost pro
rata (average cost). Every proof below is a real Tempo testnet transaction; the app's figures are its own reads
(`listOpenPositions`, `listVaultBets`, `freshExitQuote` — what the terminal and /portfolio render), checked against the
chain's receipts (`OrderExecuted`, the vault's booked fills) and balances (`positionsOf`, `positionOf`). Amounts are
pathUSD base units (6 decimals: 1,000,000 = $1); contracts likewise.
