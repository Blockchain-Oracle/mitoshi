A mention only ever trades under a grant your account made, so nothing waits in limbo: what your X calls won sits in that account's Trading Balance and X balance.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Prove it's you

Open <AppLink href="/claim">X recovery</AppLink> and **Sign in with X**. The card then reads "Your X routes to 0x…" with what waits there.
</Step>
<Step>
### Open your account

**Sign in as** that account — its passkey or wallet. It's all in [Portfolio](/trading/portfolio). Only you can cash out.
</Step>
</Steps>

If nothing routes to your X account yet, the page says so and points to [Trade from X](/x/trade-from-x).

## Share-to-sign links

A link like `/go/t/BTC/5m?side=up&stake=5` opens the terminal on that Window with the side and stake set. Nothing is placed until the person who opens it signs with their own passkey.
