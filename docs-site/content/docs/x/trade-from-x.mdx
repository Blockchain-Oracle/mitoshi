<AppLink href="/trade-from-x">Trade from X</AppLink> takes three steps: [sign in with X and link your account](/x/sign-in), fund X trading, post.

## Fund X trading

Pick $5, $10 or $25 and **Enable X trading**. One transaction from your account moves it into your Trading Balance as the **X balance**: an EXECUTOR grant to Mitoshi's X relay.

- It opens positions you own, on up to 8 Windows at once, for 30 days, from the X balance only.
- It can't withdraw or send money anywhere. **Cash out** returns what is left to your Trading Balance at any time; **Renew** restarts an expired one.

## Post a call

```
@Mitoshi_app BTC up 5m $5
@Mitoshi_app $TSLA down 15m $10
@Mitoshi_app nvidia long 1h $25
```

One asset, one side, one Window and one stake, in any order:

| Part | Accepted |
| --- | --- |
| Asset | BTC, ETH, SOL or a listed stock — a ticker, a cashtag (`$TSLA`) or a name (bitcoin, tesla, nvidia) |
| Side | up, down — or long, short, yes, no, over, under, higher, lower |
| Window | 5m, 15m or 1h |
| Stake | `$5`, `5`, `5 usd` |

A second side, asset, Window or stake is refused, not guessed; so is anything like `3x`. **Make your call** on the page builds the exact text, checks a Window is open for it, and copies it or opens X. Nothing is placed from the page.

## What the relay does

It places the call on the Window of that asset and length that is trading when you posted — a coin's 24/7 lane, a stock's 24/7 lane, else its NYSE lane (AMD, PLTR); never the Gap. A call read more than one Window late is refused ("Too late"), never placed on a later Window. Each mention is handled once.

The bot replies to calls — filled or refused, with the reason and a fix — with a receipt card; a mention that isn't a call gets no reply. Every call you made shows under **Receipts**, a fill linked to its transaction.
