## Sign in with X

On <AppLink href="/trade-from-x">Trade from X</AppLink>, **Sign in with X** sends you to X to approve `tweet.read` and `users.read` — nothing that posts. Mitoshi reads your profile once and drops X's token. The sign-in is single-use and bound to the browser that started it.

In the phone app, the X sign-in opens in the system browser and one tap brings you back to the app.

## Link your account

**Link @you** asks your passkey or wallet to sign a short text naming the network, your X account and your Mitoshi account, ending "Mentions of the Mitoshi bot may trade from my X trading balance only." From then on a call that X account posts trades for that Mitoshi account only.

- One X account routes to one Mitoshi account. If it already routes elsewhere, the page offers to sign in as that account instead.
- **Unlink X** is another signature. Your money stays where it is.
- <AppLink href="/settings/account">Account & keys</AppLink> shows the linked X account under Security.

On 13 October a passkey-shaped account's signature over that text verified, and the same signature for another X account did not ([evidence](/help/evidence)).
