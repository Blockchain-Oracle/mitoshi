## In one paragraph

Mitoshi 見通し — "see where it's going" — is a prediction market and a home for stocks on [Tempo](https://tempo.xyz). Each **Window** is one asset over a fixed stretch of time: UP wins if it closes at or above where it opened, DOWN if below. Every Window has its own order book on chain, settles on RedStone's signed prints, and pays a winning contract $1. You sign up with a passkey, take testnet dollars, and tap. It runs on **Tempo testnet (Moderato)**; the money is testnet pathUSD with no value, and everything else — books, prints, settlement, exits, receipts — is real.
