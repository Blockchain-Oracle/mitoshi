<AppLink href="/games">Games</AppLink> is a hall. Every number on it is a real record; a read that fails hides, never shows a made-up figure.

<GuideCapture name="gamesPhone" />

## The hall

- **Now playing** — a strip of the venue's fills from the last ten minutes and the arcade's newest replayed runs, a new best flagged.
- **Next live Window** — the featured cabinet: the Window [Beat the coin](/games/practice-and-arcade) would deal next, its countdown, its price and the crowd's lean, and **Play**.
- **Cabinets** — every game with its scene (the two arcade cabinets play themselves on real prices), a sticker for whose money is at risk, one live fact and **Play**.
- **Streaks** — your best trading streak and win rate, your arcade bests and your Lucky streak. Signed out, one line to sign in.
- **Achievements** — badges from duels, arcade runs and Lucky draws; a locked one names its goal.

| Game | Sticker | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <AppLink href="/games/practice">Practice</AppLink> · Beat the coin | Free | Call up to five live prices against a coin flip |
| <AppLink href="/games/line-rider">Line Rider</AppLink> · <AppLink href="/games/candle-hop">Candle Hop</AppLink> | Free | Arcade runs on the real prints |
| <AppLink href="/games/lucky">Lucky</AppLink> | Your stake | Reels deal a live call; you place it |
| <AppLink href="/games/duel">Duel</AppLink> | Your stake | Two players, one sealed deck, real calls, a side-pot |
| <AppLink href="/games/range">Range</AppLink> · <AppLink href="/games/moonshot">Moonshot</AppLink> | House pays | [House tickets](/trading/range-and-moonshot) on a band or a level |

All seven are built. A game plays full screen on a thin rail — back to the hall, settings, sound — with **How to play** behind ⓘ. Settings (sound, music, haptics, motion, accent) stay on this device.

## Ranks and history

<AppLink href="/games/rank">Rank</AppLink> holds the boards: Duel's ladder, Line Rider, Candle Hop, streaks and Lucky. <AppLink href="/games/history">History</AppLink> lists your runs, draws and duels.
