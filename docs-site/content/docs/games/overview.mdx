<AppLink href="/games">Games</AppLink> is a hall of modes built on the live markets. Every card carries the same honest label the write paths enforce: a mode whose economic kind is "none" can never send a transaction.

<GuideCapture name="gamesPhone" />

| Mode | Group | Whose money | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <AppLink href="/games/range">Range</AppLink> | Prediction | A band priced by the house | Live |
| <AppLink href="/games/moonshot">Moonshot</AppLink> | Prediction | A reach priced by the house | Live |
| <AppLink href="/games/practice">Practice</AppLink> | Duel | No stake | Live |
| <AppLink href="/games/line-rider">Line Rider</AppLink> | Arcade | Arcade score, not on chain | Live |
| <AppLink href="/games/candle-hop">Candle Hop</AppLink> | Arcade | Arcade score, not on chain | Live |
| <AppLink href="/games/duel">Duel</AppLink> | Duel | Market picks plus a side-pot | Not connected — waits on the arena contracts |
| <AppLink href="/games/lucky">Lucky</AppLink> | Prediction | One real order on the book | Not connected — waits on the draw server |

A mode that is not connected says so on its card and its page, and names what it waits on. Its rules are kept in the shared game core; nothing about it is simulated.

- [Range and Moonshot](/trading/range-and-moonshot) are house tickets on a real Window and settle on its signed print.
- [Practice and the arcade](/games/practice-and-arcade) move no money at all.

The games' own stage — full screen, a 3-2-1 count-in and a game-over card — and Lucky and Duel are the slice running now (R4-9, D-227); see [availability](/help/availability).
