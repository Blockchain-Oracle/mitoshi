## Practice

Five live Windows come as cards. Swipe up if you think the price goes above its line, down if below; a bot that is a coin flip plays the same hand.

Practice cannot wait for a Window to settle, so it asks a narrower question and says so: every card records the live public price when you swipe it, and all five are scored together on the same feed after a **30-second watch**. Every price is a real reading; nothing is simulated. It is not a settlement, a payout, a position or a real opponent, and nothing is staked.

## The arcade

**Line Rider** and **Candle Hop** are seeded, fixed-step runs drawn from a seed you can see.

- **Line Rider:** a line scrolls in from the right; drag, scroll or hold the arrow keys to keep the dot on it. Hugging the line builds a combo and refills your grip; drifting off drains it.
- **Candle Hop:** candles scroll in; tap or press Space to hop through each gap.

No stake and nothing on the ledger. Your inputs are recorded as a trace, and the server **replays the run** from the seed before it records the score: a forged score is refused, and an unsigned post is refused. The board shows the top ten and your rank.

On 12 October a live check posted a real run that ranked first, and saw the forged and unsigned posts refused ([evidence](/help/evidence)).
