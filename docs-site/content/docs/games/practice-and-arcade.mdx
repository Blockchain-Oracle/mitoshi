## Beat the coin

<AppLink href="/games/practice">Practice</AppLink> deals up to five live Windows as cards: "will BTC be higher in 30 s?" Swipe ↑ for higher, ↓ for lower. A coin flip picks the same cards. Thirty seconds after your last swipe every card is scored on the live price, and the round says who won — then **Call it for real** or **Play a real duel**.

It is not a Window's settlement, a position or a real opponent, and nothing is staked. Every price is a real reading.

## The arcade

**Line Rider** and **Candle Hop** ride a tape of the venue's own signed prints: the opening and closing prints of the last twelve hours on BTC or ETH, as the run's seed picks.

- **Line Rider:** sled the chart — rises climb, drops fall, gaps open. Hold to press, let go to hop, lean ← → to flip.
- **Candle Hop:** fly the candles, each pair a print, up or down. Tap or Space to lift; a body, a wick or the floor ends it.

A run is full screen: 3-2-1-GO, the score and your best, pause, and a game-over card with its rank, **Share**, the seed and the length. A calmer ramp is offered and marked on the board.

## Scores the server checks

The server issues each run's seed with its tape. Your inputs go back as a trace, and the server **replays the run** on that seed and tape before it records the score: a forged score, an unsigned post, or a seed already used or expired is refused. No stake, nothing on chain. The board shows the top ten on this engine build and your rank; signed out, your best stays on this device.

On 12 October a live check posted a real run that ranked first, and saw the forged and unsigned posts refused ([evidence](/help/evidence)).
