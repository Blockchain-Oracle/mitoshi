## Copy

<Steps>
<Step>
### Pick a strategy

Open a card on <AppLink href="/strategies">Automate</AppLink>. The drawer shows its market, its envelope, its fee, its record and its recent copy trades.
</Step>
<Step>
### Follow or fade

**Follow** takes the side the strategy calls. **Fade** takes the opposite side of every call — you win when it loses.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set your limits

**Budget** is what you set aside from your account for it; **Most per trade** can't be more than the budget or the strategy's own cap.
</Step>
<Step>
### Sign once

**Copy this strategy** (or **Fade this strategy**) is one transaction your passkey (or wallet) signs: approve the deposit, put the budget in your Trading Balance with a STRATEGY grant to the runner, and subscribe. A fee, if the creator set one, is paid to the creator once, when the subscription starts.
</Step>
</Steps>

From then on the runner places for you on the strategy's trigger, through your grant, from your budget. Every copy fill is yours, in your Trading Balance; settled Windows pay into it.

## Stop

Stopping is one transaction — revoke the grant and unsubscribe — and the relay pays for it. Unspent budget stays in your Trading Balance to withdraw. On 11 October a copy started in one self-paid transaction and stopped in one the deployed relay paid for; across five settled Windows the copier's P&L on the desk equalled the vault index's, cent for cent ([evidence](/help/evidence)).

## Build one

**Build a strategy** walks four steps:

1. **Its instinct** — momentum (with the move from the opening print), reversion (against it), an agent with a brief, or a mirror of a wallet's public calls.
2. **Tune it** — one series or any Window, a trigger in basis points, the agent's brief and gate.
3. **Hard limits** — most per trade, most per day, Windows at once, the dearest price, and the fee to copy. They are written into the strategy on chain for good; every copier's grant must fit inside them.
4. **Name it and publish** — optionally with an ERC-8004 identity, run by the house runner or by your own key.

A mirror copy lands after the trader's trade, at whatever the book holds then — never at their price, and sometimes not at all. Publishing metadata needs your passkey itself; a one-tap key is refused.
