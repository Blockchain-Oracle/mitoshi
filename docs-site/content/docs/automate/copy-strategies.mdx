<AppLink href="/strategies">Automate</AppLink> has three views: **Create**, **Copy a strategy** and **Your strategies**. Each card shows its net over settled trades, its ERC-8004 id and sealed memory if it has them, its fee and its copiers.

## Copy

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open a card

The drawer has four tabs: **Copy**, **Your copy**, **Decisions** and **Memory**. Its head names the limits: "$5 / trade · $50 / day · 3 open · ≤ 90¢".
</Step>
<Step>
### Follow or fade

**Follow** takes the side the strategy calls. **Fade** takes the opposite side of every call — you win when it loses.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set your budget

**Budget** is what goes into your Trading Balance for the runner; **Per trade** can't be more than the budget or the strategy's own cap. Already copying on the same runner? **Use my budget** shares it; **New budget** starts one, and the drawer names the copies it keeps and any it stops — the vault holds one strategy grant per owner.
</Step>
<Step>
### Sign once

**Copy · $…** (or **Fade · $…**) is one transaction your passkey or wallet signs: the deposit and a STRATEGY grant to the runner for 30 days (unless you share a budget), and the subscription. A fee, if the creator set one, goes to the creator once, at the start.
</Step>
</Steps>

From then on the runner places for you on the strategy's trigger, through your grant, from your budget. Every copy fill is yours, in your Trading Balance; settled Windows pay into it.

## Manage and stop

**Your copy** shows your result, the fee paid, and what is left today. **Switch to fade** (or follow) is free; **Renew its budget** sets a fresh one through the same form. **Hold to stop** revokes the grant and unsubscribes in one transaction the relay pays for; unspent budget stays in your Trading Balance. On 11 October a copy started in one self-paid transaction and stopped in one the deployed relay paid for; across five settled Windows the copier's P&L equalled the vault index's, cent for cent ([evidence](/help/evidence)).

## Build one

**Create** walks four steps:

1. **Identity & approach** — a name, a portrait, and an approach: momentum (rides the move from the open), reversion (bets it snaps back), an agent (a model reads, a gate decides) or a mirror of one wallet's calls.
2. **Behavior & limits** — the market or any Window, the minimum move, the agent's brief and gate; per trade, per day, and a risk mode: Guarded, Balanced, Active or your own. The limits are written on chain for good; every copier's grant must fit inside them.
3. **Test read** — runs the rule on a live Window now and shows what it would call. You may continue untested.
4. **Publish** — run by Mitoshi or by your own runner's key, with a fee to copy, and optionally an ERC-8004 identity (sponsored) created first.

A mirror copy lands after the trader's trade, at whatever the book holds then — never at their price, and sometimes not at all. **Copy this trader** on a profile opens Create with a mirror of that wallet. Publishing needs your passkey itself; a one-tap key is refused.
