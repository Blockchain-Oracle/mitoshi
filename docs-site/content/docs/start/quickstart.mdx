A **call** is UP or DOWN on one **Window**: one asset, one start, one close. UP wins if the closing print is at or above the opening print; DOWN wins if it is below. A winning contract pays $1.00.

<Walkthrough name="first-call-demo" />

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the terminal

Open <AppLink href="/trade/BTC">the terminal</AppLink>. Signed out, it trades on paper: the equity pill reads **DEMO**, with 10,000 paper dollars kept in this browser. A paper call fills at the live book's price and settles on the real signed result. Nothing reaches the chain.
</Step>
<Step>
### Create an account

Tap **Sign in**, then **Create account**. Face ID, a fingerprint or a security key makes a passkey, and your Tempo account comes from it — no seed phrase, no extension, no gas token. Test dollars arrive while you sign up.

Already have a wallet? Tempo Wallet, a browser wallet and WalletConnect work too. See [your account](/start/account).
</Step>
<Step>
### Turn on one-tap

After your first trade asks your passkey, a toast offers **Turn on** (it is also in Trade settings). One passkey prompt now, and every tap after it signs itself, up to $25 a day for 24 hours. Skip it and each trade asks your passkey instead. See [one-tap trading](/start/one-tap).
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick a Window and tap

The asset chip switches markets; the Window chip shows the lane and its clock ("trades 2:32"). The card on the chart shows your size, what UP and DOWN pay per dollar, the fees (free) and the trailing distance.

Tap **UP** or **DOWN**. The position shows the moment the trade is sent — "Confirming on Tempo…" — and the receipt replaces it with what the engine booked.
</Step>
<Step>
### Close it or hold it

The position's value is what **CLOSE** pays right now. Close any time, set take-profit, stop-loss or a trail ([exits](/trading/exits)), or hold to the close: the Window settles on its signed closing print and the settler pays winners a few minutes later ([settlement](/trading/settlement)).
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Testnet">
Mitoshi runs on Tempo testnet (Moderato). Testnet pathUSD has no value and can't be bought or sold. The books, prints, settlement and receipts are real contracts on a real chain.
</Callout>
