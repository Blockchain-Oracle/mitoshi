## Sign up with a passkey

**Create account** makes a passkey with Face ID, a fingerprint or a security key. Your device — or your password manager — keeps its private key. Mitoshi stores only the public key and its credential id, and your Tempo account address comes from it. Next time, **I have a passkey** signs you in with the same biometric.

A session cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days. It backs the faucet claim, the live stream's ticket and your posts in rooms; a wallet without a passkey signs once for a 30-day social session before its first post. Your account's authority stays on chain, with your passkey.

## Other ways in

If you already have a wallet, sign in with **Tempo Wallet**, a **browser wallet** or **WalletConnect**. Trading works the same way. Of the two ways to tap with no prompt, the wallet key needs a passkey account; a Trading Balance session works with any of them ([one-tap](/start/one-tap)).

## A backup passkey

From <AppLink href="/settings/account">Account & keys</AppLink>, **Add a backup passkey** on a second device, then confirm it with your first. The backup is an admin key on your Tempo account: it signs in alone as the same account. On 11 October a fresh browser holding only the backup signed in to the same account and balance ([evidence](/help/evidence)).

## Money

- Everything is **pathUSD**, Tempo's dollar stablecoin: stakes, payouts and the network fee.
- **Test dollars.** Signing up funds the new account from Moderato's faucet. After that, **Add money** — the **+** beside your balance — claims again once a day per account and says when you can. It also shows your address, with Copy and a QR code to receive pathUSD.
- Your **wallet** is your account itself. Your **Trading Balance** is a vault you own on Tempo that holds money and positions for you, so a capped key can tap from it and a keeper can run your exits ([Trading Balance](/trading/trading-balance)). Only you can withdraw from it.
- **Withdraw** sends pathUSD to any Tempo address: one transfer your passkey signs and pays the small fee for — never a one-tap key.

## See and stop every key

<AppLink href="/settings/account">Account & keys</AppLink> reads your account's whole history from Tempo. Its first strip counts your keys, the grants that can spend and what is left today, with **Hold to stop all**. **Security** lists your backup passkey, every **trading key** (what it may still approve today, when it was last used), every **Trading Balance grant** — tap-trading, reply trading from X, strategy agent, exits with the app closed — with its caps, your linked Robinhood Chain wallet and your [X account](/x/sign-in). **Revoke** or **Stop** ends one on chain; revoking is free. None of them can withdraw.

**Device**, **Data** and **Region** hold sound and notifications, your data's export and deletion, and the region check.

<Callout title="No value" type="warn">
Testnet pathUSD has no value. Never send real funds to a testnet address.
</Callout>
