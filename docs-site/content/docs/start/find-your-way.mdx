## The six places

| Key | Place | What you do there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | <AppLink href="/trade">Trade</AppLink> | One tap UP or DOWN on a live chart. |
| 2 | <AppLink href="/markets">Markets</AppLink> | Every live Window, coin and stock. Each ticker's hub is at `/tickers/<symbol>`. |
| 3 | <AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink> | Your money, positions and record. |
| 4 | <AppLink href="/games">Games</AppLink> | Every market-powered game. |
| 5 | <AppLink href="/strategies">Automate</AppLink> | Strategies and agents that trade for you. |
| 6 | <AppLink href="/leaderboard">Leaderboard</AppLink> | The strongest verified records. |

On a desk they sit in the rail on the left. On a phone the dock holds Trade, Markets, the 見通し seal, Portfolio and **More**.

## More

**More** lists everything that is not a place, grouped:

- **More ways to trade:** Parlay, Earn, Themes, Launch, Bridge, Reserves, Market Surface, Sensei, X-trade.
- **Learn:** Demo, News, How it works, Download, Reels, Docs (these guides), Reference (the app's own short pages), Pitch.
- **Games:** Practice, Duel, Lucky, Range, Moonshot, Line Rider, Candle Hop.
- **Automate:** Agents.
- **Records and proof:** Activity, Trader Edge, Stats, Game ranks, Games history, Status, Trading halts.
- **Account:** Account & keys, Notifications, X recovery.

A few of these are registered but not built yet; each says so and names what it waits on rather than show invented numbers. [Availability](/help/availability) lists them.

| Keys | Does |
| --- | --- |
