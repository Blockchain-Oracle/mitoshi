Ops is one Node process on Mitoshi's Coolify server, at `ops.testnet.mitoshi.xyz`. Every actor runs on the same loop (`runActor`): a pass every few seconds, a heartbeat, a one-line reason for what it did or why it waited. Each actor that signs has its **own role key**; an actor with no key runs dry. Dry run is the default until it is switched off, actor by actor.

## The indexer

The indexer follows the engine, the three Trading Balance vaults, the strategy registry, the ERC-8004 reputation registry and the bridge adapters, block by block, into Postgres — Windows, orders, fills, prints, settlements, vault executions and grants, and the adapters' mints, burns and held credits. At 08:30 UTC on 13 October it had seen 62,023 Windows opened and 61,946 resolved (`evidence/ops-health-2026-10-13.json`). Web reads it for Markets, Portfolio, Stats, the leaderboard and profiles.

**Recount.** A separate replay — a GitHub workflow, `recount.yml` — walks the engine's and every vault's logs and compares totals with the index. Through block 39,012,040 the recount equalled the index, total for total and Window for Window.

## The stream

Ops serves one live stream to the terminal: public topics (prices, ticks, sessions, halts) are open, and your own topic needs a 60-second ticket the web signs. A refused ticket keeps the public stream.

## Health

`/health` lists every actor's last pass, its reason, its failures and whether it is dry; <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink> shows it in the app as one board with the relay's state, and <AppLink href="/status/halts">the halts board</AppLink> keeps 30 days of halts. The container's health check uses `/livez`, so an outage at RedStone doesn't take the ops API down.
