Mitoshi's contracts are Solidity, built and tested with Foundry against Tempo's own EVM rules, and kept under 23.5 KB each. Their ABIs reach the app only through generated code (`packages/clients`), and their addresses only through the [deployment fragments](/architecture/deployments).

## The market engine

| Contract | Holds and enforces |
| --- | --- |
| **EventsEngine** | Every Window's order book, positions and state; `placeOrder`, `cancelOrders`, `recordPrint`, `settle`, `voidExpired`, `redeem`. Ported from Agari's events engine and rewritten for Tempo. One ABI, frozen as `engine-abi-v1`. |
| AdminLib · MatchLib · ResolveLib | Libraries the engine delegatecalls: listing and series, matching, and prints and settlement. |
| **EngineLens** | Read-only views: book levels, positions, Window state. |
| **RedStonePrintVerifier** | Admits a RedStone package as a print: three of five production signers, close to the boundary. |
| DataStreamsPrintVerifier · AttestedPrintVerifier | The Chainlink Data Streams path (the stocks' Gap on mainnet) and an attested-print path. |

How the engine is built:

- **One book per Window**, 1,000 price levels a side, held in bitmaps; order nodes come from one recycled pool, so a fresh order rarely pays for new storage.
- **Positions** are free and locked UP and DOWN contracts plus credit. A pair of UP and DOWN merges back to $1; a fill can mint a pair from two buyers.
- **Takers are immediate-or-cancel**, with caps on fills and evictions per call. Nothing can take before the opening print.
- **No function takes a payout address:** a payout goes to the caller, or to the holder when a settler redeems for them. Accounting never reads the token balance, so a donation can be swept but never counted.

## Products on the engine

| Contract | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **EventVaultV2** | The [Trading Balance](/trading/trading-balance): owners' accounts and grants (SESSION, EXECUTOR, STRATEGY, EXIT). |
| **FairValueGuard** | The fair price of a Window's side at a signed RedStone spot, from the opening print, the time left and a per-series volatility. Refuses by name when an input is missing or stale. |
| **MarketMakerVault** | The maker vault [Earn](/trading/earn) supplies. |
| **RangeReserve** · **ParlayReserve** · **LeverageReserve** | The house reserves behind Range and Moonshot, Parlay, and Boost. RangeReserve was redeployed on 13 October to price every Series on its own or the guard's volatility. |
| **PrivateDesk** | Link-private bets from a separate balance. |
| **GameArena** | [Duels](/games/duel-and-lucky): each player's pot, a committed deck, every pick as a real order it holds on the engine, and the award. Ported from Masayume's arena. |
| **StrategyRegistry** | Strategies and themes, their envelopes, fees, runners and ERC-8004 ids. Never touches money. |

## Pillar two

The launchpads (**LaunchRegistry**, **CurveLaunchpad**, **LiquidityLocker**) and the replayed Uniswap v4 their graduates trade on. Stocks are Windows on the engine, and a theme is a strategy on **StrategyRegistry**; the stock mirrors, their guard and pools, and the first themes' registry and router are retired (D-231, `contracts/deployments/retired/`). See [stocks and tokens](/stocks/overview).

## Verified source

The first Trading Balance, the maker vault, Parlay, Range, Boost, the private desk, StrategyRegistry, LaunchRegistry, LiquidityLocker and CurveLaunchpad were verified on Tempo's Sourcify (contracts.tempo.xyz) as they were deployed; the acceptance records note each match. The engine and EventVaultV2 have no such record.
