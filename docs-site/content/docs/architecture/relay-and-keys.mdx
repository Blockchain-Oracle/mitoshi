## Fee sponsorship

Tempo transactions can carry a separate **fee payer**: the sender signs the call, and another account signs to pay its network fee in a stablecoin. Mitoshi's relay, at `/api/relay` on the web app, is that fee payer. It can't change your call — your signature covers it — and it can't spend from your account.

The relay sponsors a call only if a **policy** for that contract allows it. There is one policy per deployed area — the engine, both Trading Balances, the maker vault, Range, Parlay, Boost, the private desk, strategies, themes, launch, stocks, agents and keys — and CI fails if a deployed area has none. Money **coming in** is never sponsored: deposits, `depositAndGrant`, grants and supply are always paid by you.

Its gates, at deployment:

| Gate | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Per sender | 120 sponsored calls an hour |
| Per device | 240 an hour, $5 of fees a day |
| Everyone together | $500 of fees a day |
| Breaker | Stops sponsoring when the fee payer's balance is under $20 |

Budgets are kept in Postgres; the last verdicts show on <AppLink href="/status">Status</AppLink>.

## Access keys

Tempo accounts can authorize **access keys** with limits and scopes enforced by the chain (TIP-1011).

- **The one-tap key** may call the engine's `placeOrder`, `cancelOrders`, `cancelAll`, `redeem` and `withdrawCredit`, and approve pathUSD to the engine only, with **$25 of new approvals a day** for **24 hours**. The limit counts each approval, so it bounds what the key can spend; selling what the account already holds on the engine needs no approval. Anything else it signs is refused `CallNotAllowed`.
- **A backup passkey** is an admin key on the account, added from Account & keys and confirmed with the first passkey. It signs in alone as the same account.
- **A theme follow** authorizes the theme agent's key, scoped to the theme router.

Keys can't create contracts: Tempo refuses a contract creation signed by an access key. Every key is listed, with its remaining limit and its spends, in Account & keys, and revoking it is free.

## Grants are not keys

Trading Balance grants (SESSION, STRATEGY, EXIT) are permissions inside the vault contract, not keys on your account. A SESSION grant's actor is still a key made on your device; a STRATEGY or EXIT grant's actor is a Mitoshi runner or keeper. See [the Trading Balance](/trading/trading-balance).
