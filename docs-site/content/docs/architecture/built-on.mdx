Mitoshi runs on Tempo, a payments-first Layer 1 incubated by Stripe and Paradigm. Each row says what a platform does for the product and where it stands on testnet.

| Platform | What it does for Mitoshi | On testnet today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tempo** | The chain: every contract, every dollar (pathUSD, a TIP-20 stablecoin) and every network fee. Passkeys are accounts, access keys carry limits the chain enforces, another account can pay a call's fee, and the ERC-8004 registries and MPP are built in. | Live on Moderato (chain 42431). Its faucet gives each new account test dollars. |
| **RedStone** | The signed prints that settle every Window — three of five production signers — plus the fair-value guard's spot and the stock pools' reference. | Live. Its public gateways go dark in growing daily windows and stop on 29 October 2026; the authenticated ones wait on a key ([availability](/help/availability)). |
| **LayerZero** | The bridge: a Stock Token locked on Robinhood Chain mints its mirror on Tempo, and back. | Moderato → Base Sepolia delivered in 20 s. The return waits on LayerZero's testnet verifier. |
| **Robinhood Chain** | Home of Robinhood's Stock Tokens: the lockboxes and the split-and-pause sender go there. | Not deployed yet: the deployer has no testnet ETH ([availability](/help/availability)). |
| **Uniswap v4** | A pool per mirror against pathUSD, kept in a band around the share price by Mitoshi's hook. | Replayed at mainnet's addresses on Moderato; pools opened; no screen trades them yet. |

The chart's live line comes from Coinbase (crypto) and Alpaca (stocks). It settles nothing.

## Why Tempo

- **Dollars all the way down.** Stakes, payouts and network fees are all pathUSD. There is no gas token to buy first.
- **A passkey is an account.** Tempo verifies WebAuthn signatures, so Face ID or a fingerprint signs a call — no extension, no seed phrase.
- **One tap, bounded by the chain.** An access key may call the engine and approve at most $25 a day for 24 hours (TIP-1011). Anything else it signs is refused.
- **Someone else can pay the fee.** Mitoshi's relay co-signs as the fee payer. It can't change your call or spend from your account.
- **Agents and paid reads are native.** The ERC-8004 identity and reputation registries are predeployed; MPP charges per request in pathUSD.
