## Rooms and takes

Every ticker has a **room**, open from the terminal. Anyone can read it. To post, you need a call on that ticker — a direct fill or a Trading Balance execution on the engine; the room refuses everyone else ("Make a call on TSLA to post here."). **Takes** are short posts tagged by their cashtags; they show in each tagged ticker's feed and can carry a reaction.

You can name your profile, follow people, block someone (their posts leave your rooms and takes) and report a post. Reports go to a moderation queue that ops watches; a hidden post leaves every read. Your own posts can't be reported by you — edit or delete them instead.

## Alerts and notifications

**Price alerts** fire above or below a price, in NYSE hours or any hour. The notification centre collects fills, verdicts, payouts and alerts; <AppLink href="/settings/notifications">Notifications</AppLink> chooses what reaches you.

## Sensei

Sensei reads the Window on your screen with you and gives a straight call — UP, DOWN, or sit it out. It reads and recommends; **you place every call**. Before your first question it names who receives what, and asks your consent. Answers stream word by word. Not financial advice.

Sensei runs on the testnet web app with the owner's model key since 12 October. With no model configured, it says so and everything else works without it.

## Reels and News

- <AppLink href="/reels">Reels</AppLink>: one live Window per card — the question, the price against its line, a sparkline — to swipe through and open.
- <AppLink href="/news">News</AppLink>: stock headlines from Finnhub and coin news from Cointelegraph and Decrypt.

## Your data

From Account & keys you can **export** your off-chain data (profile, takes, follows) and **delete** it with a fresh signature, with an Undo. Deleting removes every off-chain row of your account and touches no one else's. What is on chain stays on chain.
