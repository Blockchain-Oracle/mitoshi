## Stats: proof of demand

<AppLink href="/stats">Stats</AppLink> counts the wallets that made a call, straight from the engine's fills: the headline count and who was active in 24 hours, calls by UTC day, then wallets, calls, what changed hands and what was paid out; the latest calls with their receipts; and the venue's own counts — Windows opened, settled and voided, prints, house fills.

- The house's keys — its maker, its taker, the maker vault — are never counted; their fills are shown apart.
- A tap from a Trading Balance counts for the wallet it was made for.
- Calls taken by Boost, Parlay, Range or the private desk count as calls; their wallets aren't named yet.
- On testnet the wallets include the fresh passkey accounts Mitoshi's own live checks create.

A recount replayed the engine and all three Trading Balances through block 39,012,040, and the index matched it total for total and Window for Window.

## Leaderboard and profiles

The <AppLink href="/leaderboard">leaderboard</AppLink> ranks wallets by what settled — P&L or return on stake; daily, weekly, monthly or all time — with each wallet's rounds, win rate and best run. A **round** is one wallet in one Window: what came back (sold before the close, plus the payout on what was held) less what went in. Each row opens the wallet's public profile at `/u/<address>`: P&L, rank, tier, followers, return, wins, rounds and best run; **Follow** and **Copy this trader**; then its record, open calls, settled Windows with their proofs, and takes. A house key reads "never ranked". The same board is in the terminal's account drawer.

## The season league

Each week from Monday 00:00 UTC, wallets earn points for calls, wins, their best run and active days, and are ranked with a tier. House keys earn nothing.

## Share cards

**Share** draws a card in your browser, with a link and its QR code:

- **A position** — from its details: the call, its P&L, its return or both.
- **A call** — an open Range, Moonshot, Parlay or Up/Down: the stake, what it wins if it lands, and when the Window closes.
- **A verdict** — from History: WON, LOST or VOID, the close print and who signed it; the QR opens the proof.
- **An arcade run** — from the game-over card: the score, a new best, its rank and seed.

**Download** saves it or opens your phone's share sheet; **Share on X** opens a post with the real figures. Terminal, proof, ticker, profile and agent pages each have their own link preview.
