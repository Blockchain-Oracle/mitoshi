## Stats: proof of demand

<AppLink href="/stats">Stats</AppLink> counts the wallets that made a call, straight from the engine's fills: calls, what changed hands, what was paid out, the growth by day, the venue's own counts and the latest calls with their receipts.

- The house's keys — its maker, its taker, the maker vault — are never counted; their fills are shown apart.
- A tap from a Trading Balance counts for the wallet it was made for.
- Calls taken by Boost, Parlay, Range or the private desk count as calls; their wallets aren't named yet.
- On testnet the wallets include the fresh passkey accounts Mitoshi's own live checks create.

The S5 gate replayed the engine and all three Trading Balances through block 39,012,040 and the index matched the recount total for total and Window for Window.

## Leaderboard and profiles

The <AppLink href="/leaderboard">leaderboard</AppLink> ranks wallets by what settled — P&L or return on stake; daily, weekly, monthly or all time — with each wallet's rounds, win rate and best run. A **round** is one wallet in one Window: what came back (sold before the close, plus the payout on what was held) less what went in. Each row opens the wallet's public profile at `/u/<address>`: its figures, tier and badges, and latest settled Windows with their proofs. The same board is in the terminal's account drawer.

## The season league

Each week from Monday 00:00 UTC, wallets earn points for calls, wins, their best run and active days, and are ranked with a tier. House keys earn nothing.

## Share cards

A position's details have **Share**: a card with the call, its P&L, its return or both, the price, and the terminal's link with a QR code, drawn in your browser. **Download** saves it or opens your phone's share sheet; **Share on X** opens a post. Every terminal and proof page has its own link preview.

<Callout>
Tiers and badge thresholds are inherited from the app Mitoshi's interface descends from. No tier pays a bonus or changes a fee.
