Every power here is enforced by a contract on Tempo testnet, not by Mitoshi's servers. Read on 13 October 2026 at block 39,348,596; the redeployed RangeReserve and the duel arena at block 39,384,844.

## Always true

- **Money leaves only to its owner.** No withdrawal or payout in the engine, the Trading Balance, Earn, Range, Parlay, Boost, the private desk or the duel arena takes a destination: each pays the account the money belongs to.
- **Signed prints decide.** A print needs three of RedStone's five production signers, a set fixed at deployment. A Window's rules freeze when it opens. Anyone can post a print, settle, or void a Window whose print never came — at half. No one can void or decide a Window by hand.
- **No upgrades.** The engine, the Trading Balance, Earn, the reserves, the private desk, the duel arena and the strategy registry are not proxies; their code can't change. Only Uniswap's position descriptor in the v4 replay sits behind a proxy.
- **Settling and withdrawing never pause.** Admins can stop new business, never claims, refunds, settlement or withdrawals.

## Who can do what

| Who | Can | Can't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You** | Everything on your account: trade, withdraw, revoke any key or grant. | — |
| **One-tap key** · 24 h | Buy on the engine with up to $25 of new approvals a day; sell, cancel and redeem what your account holds there. | Transfer, approve anyone else, call another contract. |
| **SESSION grant** · a key on your device | Buy from its budget inside its per-tap, daily, open-Window and price caps; sell what your Trading Balance holds. | Withdraw or send money. Sale proceeds stay in your Trading Balance. |
| **STRATEGY grant** · the runner a strategy or a theme names | Buy inside the strategy's envelope and your budget (a theme's call takes its stock's weight of your per-call cap). The contract also lets it sell what your Trading Balance holds; Mitoshi's house runner only buys. | Withdraw or send money. |
| **EXECUTOR grant** · Mitoshi's X relay | Buy on the Window your post on X names, from the X balance you funded, on up to 8 open Windows. The contract also lets it sell what your Trading Balance holds; the relay only buys. | Withdraw or send money, or spend past the X balance. |
| **EXIT grant** · Mitoshi's exit keeper | Sell what your Trading Balance holds, at or above the fair-value floor. | Buy, withdraw, or sell under the floor. |
| **The private desk** | Spend your private balance up to the allowance you set; carry bets and payouts through its pool. | Withdraw your balance — only you can, to yourself. |
| **The relay** | Pay your call's network fee. | Change your call or spend from your account. |
| **Mitoshi's keepers** | Open Windows, post prints, settle and pay winners, quote Earn's vault inside its public limits, settle duels, act through the grants and keys you gave. | Anything else. |

## What admins can do

| Contract | Admin can | Admin can't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Engine** | List series; add rules for Windows not yet open; switch to reduce-only (sells only) or halted (no orders); name the keys that open Windows. | Change an open Window, decide or void one, move money. Prints, settlement, redemptions, cancels and withdrawals run in every mode. |
| **Earn** | Pause new supply and quotes; set the vault's limits; name its one quoting key. | Withdraw suppliers' money. |
| **Range · Parlay · Boost** | Pause new tickets and supply; set prices and limits for new tickets; set Range's volatilities and asset tickers; set Boost's knock-out line, which open boosts follow. | Take an escrowed payout, change a ticket's payout. |
| **Private desk** | Pause new bets; set the stake band; name the desk key. | Withdraw anyone's balance. |
| **Duel arena** | Pause new duels, joins and picks; set each pot tier and its per-card cap for new duels; set the join, reveal and pick windows and the deck size. | Move a pot or a credit, decide a duel, or stop a reveal, settlement, refund or claim. A duel keeps the pot and cap it opened with. |
| **Fair-value guard** | Set each series' volatility and spot source. Exit floors, the fair-value checks on Parlay and Boost, and Range's price where the reserve has no volatility of its own follow. | Move money. |

The Trading Balance and the strategy registry have no admin.
