Mitoshi's servers open Windows, post prints, quote and pay winners. If they stopped, no new Window would open — and everything already in a contract stays yours, with no Mitoshi key needed to get it out.

## What keeps working

- **Settlement.** Anyone can post a Window's signed RedStone print and settle it. If no print arrives before the Window's deadline, anyone can void it: both sides get **$0.50** a contract.
- **Payouts.** Anyone can pay a winner — the engine's `redeemFor`, Range's and Parlay's `claim`, Boost's `settle`, the duel arena's `claimCredit` — and the money goes only to the owner.
- **Your Trading Balance.** Anyone can credit a settled Window into it (`crankSettle`); only you can withdraw.

## Getting each kind of money out

Contract addresses are on [deployments](/architecture/deployments); the explorer shows your transactions and their ids.

| Where it is | Call | Who may call |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A wallet position on the engine | `redeem(marketId)` once settled; `withdrawCredit(marketId, amount)`; `cancelAll(marketId, max, true)` for resting orders | You — `redeemFor(marketId, you)` by anyone |
| Trading Balance | `crankSettle(you, marketId)`, then `withdraw(amount)` | Anyone, then you |
| Earn | `settle(marketId)` for each Window past expiry, then `withdraw(shares)` | Anyone, then you |
| A house reserve — Range, Parlay, Boost | `withdraw(shares)`, from what isn't locked against live tickets | You |
| Range · Moonshot | `settle(roundId)`, then `claim(roundId)`; `voidStale(roundId)` if the engine never answered | Anyone |
| Parlay | `resolveLeg(parlayId, legIdx)` per leg, then `claim(parlayId)` | Anyone |
| Boost | `settle(positionId)` after the Window; `close(positionId, minProceeds)` before it | Anyone; `close` you |
| Private desk | `withdraw(amount)` — your unspent balance | You |
| A duel | `lockPicks(matchId)` after the pick window, `settleCard(matchId, card)` per card, `finalize(matchId)`, then `claimCredit(you)`; `refundUnjoined` or `refundUnrevealed` if it never started | Anyone |
| A followed theme | Nothing to get out: the holdings sit in your own account. `unfollow(themeId)` stops the agent | You |

Your ids are also listed on chain: `roundsOf`, `parlaysOf` and `positionsOf` page through an owner's tickets.

## The catches

- **A passkey belongs to its site.** Mitoshi's passkeys are made for `mitoshi.xyz`: only pages on that domain, and an app it names, can ask one to sign. Without them, payouts anyone can push still reach a passkey account, but a withdrawal needs your signature. An account from Tempo Wallet or a browser wallet signs through that wallet, without Mitoshi.
- **A private bet's payout** waits in its slot until the desk carries it home: the chain names no owner on a slot. Money you deposited and haven't bet is always yours to withdraw.
- **Earn's withdrawal** waits until every Window past expiry is settled — anyone can settle them — and pays from what isn't locked in live Windows.
