Most of it comes down to one fact: **a key that may sell may sell cheaply.** A sale's money always lands with you, but whoever bought at a bad price keeps the difference.

## If it were turned against you

| Who | The most it could do | Bounded by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your one-tap key** | Buy with up to $25 of new approvals; sell what your account holds on the engine at any price. | Its 24 hours, or until you revoke it. The key can't be copied out of your browser: misusing it takes your unlocked browser or code running on Mitoshi's site. |
| **A SESSION grant** | Spend its budget inside its caps; sell what your Trading Balance holds at any price. | The budget you set aside and the grant's expiry. Same browser-bound key. |
| **A strategy's runner** | The same, inside the strategy's envelope and your copy budget. A runner is whoever its creator named; Mitoshi's house runner only buys. | Your copy budget, what your Trading Balance holds, and the grant's expiry. |
| **Mitoshi's X relay** · and anyone who takes your X account | Spend your X balance on calls, up to 8 open Windows; the contract would also let the relay sell what your Trading Balance holds at any price. | The X balance you funded, the grant's 30 days, and **Cash out**. |
| **Mitoshi's exit keeper** | Sell what your Trading Balance holds down to its floor: fair value less the wider of 15 % or 10¢ a contract, the terms the app grants. | The contract's ceiling of 20 % or 15¢, and the grant's 30 days. |
| **A theme's agent key** | Trade your holdings through the theme router, inside the follow's per-trade, daily and slippage caps; the output always comes back to you. | Each token's daily limit on the key, the caps you set, and Unfollow. |
| **The private desk key** | Spend your private balance up to your allowance, and credit any depositor from its pool. | Your allowance; the pool holds only what was just charged or just won. |
| **An admin key** | Pause new business; set prices and limits for new tickets and rules for Windows not yet open; raise Boost's knock-out line, which open boosts follow; skew the fair-value guard behind exit floors, the Parlay and Boost checks and Range's price; change the duel arena's windows. | It can't move money, change an open Window, decide a result or stop withdrawals. |
| **The relay's fee payer** | Spend its own balance, or stop sponsoring — then each call costs you its fee. | Its balance. |
| **Mitoshi's site, if compromised** | While you have it open: use your armed keys inside their bounds, and ask your passkey to sign. A passkey prompt doesn't show what it signs. | The trust any web wallet asks of its site. |
| **RedStone's signers** | Three of the five together could sign a false price and decide Windows. | The oracle you trust for every result. |

## Keep it small

- Arm one-tap and Trading Balance sessions with small budgets, and **Stop** them in <AppLink href="/settings/account">Account & keys</AppLink> when you're done. Revoking is free.
- Copy strategies whose runner you trust; the copy drawer names it.
- Keep in the Trading Balance only what you're trading.

## Not yet done

- Every key is one key on testnet. Safes for mainnet are an open owner decision.
- No external audit is recorded. The contracts are tested with Foundry — unit, fuzz and invariant suites — and fork-tested against Moderato.
- Delegated sales on the engine have no price floor except EXIT's.

It's testnet: the money has no value. See [who can move what](/safety/who-can-do-what) and [if Mitoshi stops](/safety/if-mitoshi-stops).
