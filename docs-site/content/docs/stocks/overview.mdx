Mitoshi brings stocks to Tempo in two ways. One is live; the other is deployed on testnet with its screens live, and waits on the Robinhood side.

## 1. Call a stock — live

AAPL, AMD, AMZN, GOOGL, META, MSFT, NVDA, PLTR and TSLA trade as [Windows](/trading/windows): 5, 15 and 60 minutes in NYSE hours, the same lanes around the clock on RedStone's 24/7 feeds for seven of them, and the weekend Gap. No token changes hands — you call the price, and the Window settles on RedStone's signed print. Each ticker has a hub of its own — <AppLink href="/tickers/TSLA">TSLA's</AppLink>, for one.

## 2. Hold the real share token — the Robinhood side next

Robinhood issues **Stock Tokens** on Robinhood Chain. Mitoshi's pillar two brings them to Tempo:

<Architecture name="stocks" />

| Piece | State on testnet |
| --- | --- |
| Five mirror TIP-20s (bTSLA, bAMZN, bPLTR, bNFLX, bAMD) and their mint/burn adapters | Deployed on Moderato |
| The sync receiver for multipliers and pauses, and the scaled views | Deployed on Moderato |
| LayerZero hop through Base Sepolia (transit, composer, forwarder) | Deployed; Moderato → Base Sepolia delivered in 20 s, Base Sepolia → Moderato in 23 s |
| Lockboxes and the sync sender on Robinhood Chain testnet | Waiting on testnet ETH (owner action) |
| Uniswap v4 on Moderato, the price guard and the reference | Deployed; four pools initialized with house liquidity |
| Themes | Deployed and configured; three themes published, followed on testnet |
| `/bridge`, `/reserves` and `/themes` | Live. Until the lockboxes are deployed the bridge reads "Robinhood side not live", the reserves **Partial**, and the guarded pools — and so every theme rebalance — wait on the first stock sync |

Read on: [the bridge](/stocks/bridge), [pools and the guard](/stocks/pools-and-guard), [reserves](/stocks/reserves), [follow a theme](/stocks/follow-a-theme). The same pillar adds [Launch](/stocks/launch) for stablecoins and memecoins.
