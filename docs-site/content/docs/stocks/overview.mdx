AAPL, AMD, AMZN, GOOGL, META, MSFT, NVDA, PLTR and TSLA trade as [Windows](/trading/windows) of 5, 15 and 60 minutes in NYSE hours. Seven of them — all but AMD and PLTR — also trade around the clock on RedStone's 24/7 feeds, which pause around 04:00 ET each day. All nine have the weekend Gap.

Every Window opens and settles on RedStone's signed print (3 of 5 signers), verified on chain. The chart's live line is Alpaca's and settles nothing. No share or token changes hands: you call the price.

Each ticker has a hub of its own — <AppLink href="/tickers/TSLA">TSLA's</AppLink>, for one. Sessions, halts and earnings are in [Windows](/trading/windows).

The same section covers [themes](/stocks/follow-a-theme) and [Launch](/stocks/launch) for stablecoins and memecoins.
