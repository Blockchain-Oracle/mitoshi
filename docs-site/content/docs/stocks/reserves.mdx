## The check

For each ticker, two numbers on two chains should agree:

| Robinhood Chain | Tempo |
| --- | --- |
| What the ticker's **StockLockbox** has outstanding towards Tempo — its `outstanding` per destination, readable through `reserveView()` | The mirror's **total supply**, which only the adapter can change, by minting on arrival and burning on the way out |

By design, supply on Tempo never exceeds what was locked — the invariant tests hold it on both paths, direct and through the testnet hop: the adapter mints only on a verified LayerZero message from the lockbox's side, and a credit it can't mint is deferred rather than minted elsewhere. Tokens in flight, sent but not yet delivered, are the only honest gap, and the check will name them.

## The reserves screen

<AppLink href="/reserves">Reserves</AppLink> — **Backing** in More — reads Robinhood Chain, Base and Tempo in your browser, ticker by ticker, at stated blocks, and gives one verdict:

| Verdict | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Backed** | Every check passed: the lockbox holds what it owes, and what it owes equals the mirror's supply, held credits, the hop on Base and what is in flight |
| **Short** | A check failed; the ticker says which |
| **Partial** | A check couldn't run — it is never passed by assumption |

On Tempo alone two more checks hold: only the adapter mints — supply equals its net mints, and no one else holds the mirror's issuer role — and supply stays within the cap. **Re-check** reads every chain again in your browser. Ops' **reserve-watch** keeps a snapshot every 5 minutes and alerts when a check fails or anyone but the adapter can mint; the screen shows 30 days of it.

## Where it stands

The verdict on testnet is **Partial**: the Robinhood-side lockboxes are not deployed yet (they wait on testnet ETH, an owner action). On 13 October reserve-watch read "5 tickers partial at Tempo #39388481 · Robinhood side not deployed" (`evidence/ops-health-2026-10-13.json`). The Tempo side checks out: on 12 October, at Moderato block 39,326,334, all five mirrors had a supply of zero, nothing deferred, net mints of zero, and the adapter alone held each issuer role (`acceptance/R4-12.md`; supplies also in `evidence/mirror-supplies-2026-10-12.json`).
