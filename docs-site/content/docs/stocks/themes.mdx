A **theme** is a strategy on the **StrategyRegistry** whose spec is a `basket`: two to eight stocks with weights that add
to 100 %, the lanes it trades (market hours, 24/7, or both) and one rule — momentum or reversion with a trigger, or an
agent that reads each Window. The spec's hash and its words are on chain. [Follow a theme](/stocks/follow-a-theme) walks
the screens.

## What it trades

The house **strategy-runner** reads every Window of every stock in the basket on its lanes: the 5, 15 and 60-minute
Windows in NYSE hours, and the same lengths 24/7 on RedStone's `<T>---24_7` feeds for the seven stocks that have one
(not AMD or PLTR). On each Window the rule makes at most one call, UP or DOWN, and keeps it for that Window. Every
Window opens and settles on RedStone's signed print, like any other.

Each call is sized by the stock's weight: a 60 % stock's call takes 60 % of the most a follower lets one call stake.
A theme holds no shares and has no price of its own. Its page draws the weighted mix of its stocks' closes, which is a
view, never a follower's return.

## Follow

Following is copying the strategy: one transaction from your account grants the runner a **STRATEGY** budget on your
Trading Balance — a cap per call, a cap per day and a term — and subscribes. You pay it; the relay never sponsors a
grant. The runner can only place calls through that grant. It can't withdraw or transfer, and stopping (revoke and
unsubscribe, sponsored) ends it at once.

## Its agent

Every theme published by Mitoshi names ERC-8004 agent **#82**. Its registration file lives at
`/api/themes/agent/registration.json`, the URI its identity points to.
