A **theme** is a basket of mirrors with weights and rules, published by a creator on **ThemeRegistry**. Following it lets the theme's agent keep your account in the basket — trading inside your own account, never holding your money.

## Follow

You consent once with your passkey and caps: one transaction authorizes the agent's **access key** on your Tempo account and records the follow on **ThemeRouter**. The relay's themes policy allows exactly that pair and never the key's authorization alone; the relay engine does not honour that batch yet, so a sponsored follow is refused today (an open integration item).

The key may only call the router's `rebalance` and approve tokens to the router. Each token it can approve has its own daily limit, and an approval to anyone else is refused on chain (`CallNotAllowed`).

## Rebalance

A rebalance swaps through the [guarded pools](/stocks/pools-and-guard), pulls only the exact approvals the key raised, and sends every output to you — the router has no recipient argument. The only tokens it ever holds are creator fees owed.

## On testnet today

ThemeRegistry and ThemeRouter are deployed on Moderato and configured: the guard set, the five mirrors' pools registered, the oracle set. On a Moderato fork against the deployed contracts (with stand-ins for the guard and oracle), a follow cost 934,163 gas and the agent's key-signed rebalance bought TSLA, PLTR and NFLX through the pools. <AppLink href="/themes">Themes</AppLink> is registered and not built.
