<AppLink href="/themes">Themes</AppLink> lists stock baskets an agent rebalances. Each card shows its stocks, its weighted price and the week's move, how often it rebalances and in which hours, its followers, its creator fee and its agent — or **Paused** when its market is closed or a price is halted.

<GuideCapture name="themesDesk" />

## A theme's page

**Overview** opens on its value and the week's line; **Holdings** gives each stock's target and band; **Activity** lists its rebalances; **Rules** holds the creator fee, the slippage cap, how often it may trade, how old a price may be, its hours, its version and spec hash. Following, **You** shows what you hold and whether every stock sits inside its band.

## Follow

<Steps>
<Step>
### Set your caps

**Follow** opens the sheet: an allocation, the most per trade and per day.
</Step>
<Step>
### Sign once

"What you are signing": one key for this theme's agent. It may only rebalance through the theme router and approve it, within your daily limits — never transfer. One passkey confirmation, and the relay pays the fee. A theme of more than five stocks takes one more signature per five.
</Step>
<Step>
### Let it run

The agent keeps your account near the weights. Your holdings stay in your own account the whole time.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Unfollow** stops the agent, with five seconds to **Undo**; your holdings stay. **Hold to sell everything** sells the basket back to dollars through the same pools; you sign it and pay its fee.

## Create one

**Create** walks four steps: **Stocks** (1 to 16, with weights and cash), **Envelope** (Careful ±3 %, Balanced ±7 % or Loose ±15 % around each target, and its hours — market, plus pre and after hours, or around the clock), **Name and fee**, and **Publish**, which you sign and pay for.
