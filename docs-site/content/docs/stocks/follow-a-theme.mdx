<AppLink href="/themes">Themes</AppLink> lists stock baskets an agent trades. Each card shows its stocks and weights,
the week's move of their weighted mix and its weighted price, its rule, and whether it calls now: **Live** in market
hours, **24/7**, or **Closed** with when it trades again.

<GuideCapture name="themesDesk" />

## A theme's page

The chart draws the weighted mix over a week, a month, three months or a year. **Mix** gives each stock's weight and
the biggest call it takes. **Calls** lists the Windows the theme took a side on, each with its result. Beside them sit
its followers, its fee to follow, its biggest call, its copies' record and its rules.

## Follow

<Steps>
<Step>
### Set a budget

**Follow** opens the sheet: a budget and the most one call may stake. Each call takes its stock's weight of that.
</Step>
<Step>
### Sign once

One transaction grants the runner that budget on your Trading Balance and subscribes. You pay its fee.
</Step>
<Step>
### Let it run

The runner calls each stock's Windows by the theme's rule. Your calls show on Portfolio and settle on RedStone's
prints. **Hold to stop** ends it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Create one

**Create** walks three steps: **Stocks** (where it trades — market hours, 24/7 or all hours — then 2 to 8 stocks and
their weights), **Rule** (momentum or reversion with a trigger, or an agent with a brief), and **Publish** (a name, a
line, the biggest call, a daily cap and a fee), which you sign and pay for.

<Callout title="On testnet today">
Calls fill only where a maker quotes the Window. A stock without a 24/7 feed (AMD, PLTR) can't join a 24/7 theme. How
it all works: [themes](/stocks/themes).
</Callout>
