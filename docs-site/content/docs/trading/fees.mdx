| Fee | Who pays | How much |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trading | — | None: the engine has no trading-fee field. |
| Settlement | — | None: a winning contract pays $1.00, all of it. |
| A tap's network fee | Mitoshi's relay | Paid in pathUSD for calls inside its policy; you hold no gas token. |
| Withdrawing from the Trading Balance, revoking a key or a grant | Mitoshi's relay | — |
| Arming a Trading Balance, supplying Earn | You | A fraction of a cent; money coming in is never fee-sponsored. |
| Withdraw to another address | You | The network fee of one pathUSD transfer. |
| Range, Moonshot, Parlay | — | Priced into the stake the ticket shows before you confirm; nothing on top. |
| Boost | You, to the reserve | A premium the reserve keeps for fronting the position (8 % of what it fronts, at deployment), shown before you confirm. |
| A duel's pot and picks | You | The network fee; the relay pays for settling, refunds and claims. |
| A memecoin's curve | You, split between its creator, the template's partner and the protocol | The curve's trading fee, shown as **Fee now**: high at launch, falling to its floor. The relay pays the network fee. |
| A theme's rebalance | You | The theme's creator fee on what each rebalance trades, to the creator, and the rebalance's network fee, which the agent's key pays from your account inside its limit. |

Tempo charges network fees in a stablecoin, so there is no gas token to hold. Which calls the relay sponsors is listed per contract in [the relay](/architecture/relay-and-keys).
