The Trading Balance is **EventVaultV2**, one contract that keeps an account for every owner: free money, money set aside in grants, and the positions it holds. Its point is delegation you can bound: you give a key or a keeper one narrow power, and the contract enforces the caps.

## Add and withdraw

- **Add** moves pathUSD from your account in one passkey confirmation. You pay that fee — a fraction of a cent; deposits are never fee-sponsored.
- **Withdraw** returns free money to your own account, any time. The relay pays that fee.
- Settled Windows pay into the Trading Balance on their own — no prompt, no claim.

## Grants

Each owner holds at most one live grant of each kind. Every grant names one actor and expires.

| Grant | Who acts | What it may do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SESSION** | A key made on your device | Buy UP or DOWN from a budget you set aside, up to a per-tap and a daily cap, a number of open Windows and a dearest price; sell what the Trading Balance holds ([one-tap](/start/one-tap)) |
| **STRATEGY** | A strategy's runner | Buy for you inside the strategy's envelope and your own budget. The contract also lets it sell what the Trading Balance holds; Mitoshi's house runner only buys ([copy strategies](/automate/copy-strategies)) |
| **EXIT** | Mitoshi's exit keeper | Sell positions the Trading Balance holds, at or above a fair-value floor; no budget, no buys ([exits](/trading/exits)) |
| **EXECUTOR** | Reply trading from X | Defined in the contract; its screens are not built |

No grant can withdraw or send money anywhere. Proceeds of every sale land back in the owner's account in the vault. Daily caps count by UTC day.

## Stop

<AppLink href="/settings/account">Account & keys</AppLink> lists every grant with what it may still spend. **Stop** revokes it on chain in one transaction the relay pays for; a SESSION grant's unspent budget returns to your free balance.

## Previous Trading Balances

The Trading Balance moved twice on testnet: from the first `EventVault` to EventVaultV2 (exits with the app closed), then to a redeployed V2 when the exit floor gained its absolute slack in cents. Both earlier vaults stay indexed, cranked and relay-sponsored for withdraw, revoke and settlement; the account drawer shows what you hold in one so you can take it out. Their addresses are in [deployments](/architecture/deployments).
