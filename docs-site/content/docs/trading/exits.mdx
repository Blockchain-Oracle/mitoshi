## CLOSE and Reduce

**CLOSE** sells your whole position back to the Window's book in one tap, at what the position shows — or a little below it, within the close tolerance in Trade settings. **Reduce** sells 25, 50 or 75 %. If the book has moved further than the tolerance, the sale asks again at the new price rather than fill worse. "Closing…" shows the instant the sale is signed. A Window that has locked can't be sold into; it settles.

## Take-profit, stop-loss, trail

- **Take-profit** sells when what CLOSE pays rises to your target.
- **Stop-loss** sells when it falls to your floor.
- **Trail** arms once the price is the trailing distance past break-even. Its stop only moves your way, and it closes when the price comes back to it.

On a wallet position these run in your browser while Mitoshi is open, and a trail needs one-tap on to sell without a prompt. A Boost closes from its own row with your passkey; trail and TP/SL don't run on it.

## With the app closed

On a [Trading Balance](/trading/trading-balance) position, **one passkey confirmation** grants Mitoshi's keeper an **EXIT** permission. When your exit triggers, the keeper sells whether your tab is open or not.

<Architecture name="exits" />

- **The floor.** Every keeper sale's limit must be at or above the Window's fair value at a signed RedStone spot, less the wider of 15 % of it or 10¢ — the allowance the app grants. The contract refuses anything wider than 20 % or 15¢, and a sale under the floor reverts.
- **No bid, no sale.** If no bid meets the floor, the keeper waits and tries again until the Window locks, rather than sell lower. The terminal shows its last reason ("Keeper waiting: …").
- **Where it applies.** The floor comes from the fair-value guard, which has a volatility and a signed spot for every series, crypto and stocks. It refuses to price a Window it has no model for, and with no floor the keeper cannot sell.
- **The house's side.** The house keeps its exit side quoted until about 30 seconds before a Window locks.

Every EXIT grant lasts 30 days and is listed in <AppLink href="/settings/account">Account & keys</AppLink>; **Stop** revokes it on chain.
