Open a proof from a position's details (**Proof of the prints**) or at `/proof/<marketId>`. It shows the Window's verdict and settlement transaction, and both prints with their signers and the transaction that recorded each.

## Re-verify in this browser

<Steps>
<Step>
### Read the record

Your browser reads the print's record transaction straight from Tempo's RPC.
</Step>
<Step>
### Take the packages out

It decodes the RedStone signed packages from that transaction's calldata.
</Step>
<Step>
### Ask the verifier

It calls `verifyPrint` on the same on-chain verifier the engine used, with `eth_call`, and compares the price it returns with what the engine recorded.
</Step>
</Steps>

Nothing in that check comes from Mitoshi's servers. The page says whether it matches.

## Check against the archive

Ops' archive keeps RedStone's own packages for every boundary instant, apart from any transaction. **Check against the archive** runs the same verifier on them. For a Window that never got a print, it shows what the archive held and why the verifier refused it — too few signers at that instant, for example.

## What it does not prove

A proof shows that a recorded print is the signed price the verifier accepts. It does not say the house quoted fairly, or that a position was yours: those are on the [record](/explore/record) and in your own receipts.
