Open a proof from a position's details, from <AppLink href="/proof">Print proofs</AppLink> — every settled Window, newest first — or at `/proof/<marketId>`. It shows the Window's verdict, a timeline (opened, open print, close print, settled) and both prints with their signers and the transaction that recorded each.

## Re-verify in this browser

<Walkthrough name="proof-reverify" />

**Re-verify**, in the page head, runs every check at once:

<Steps>
<Step>
### Read the record

Your browser reads the print's record transaction straight from Tempo's RPC.
</Step>
<Step>
### Take the packages out

It decodes the RedStone signed packages from that transaction's calldata.
</Step>
<Step>
### Ask the verifier

It calls `verifyPrint` on the same on-chain verifier the engine used, with `eth_call`, and compares the price it returns with what the engine recorded.
</Step>
</Steps>

The same press also runs the verifier on ops' archive — RedStone's own packages for every boundary instant, kept apart from any transaction. For a Window that never got a print, it shows what the archive held and why the verifier refused it — too few signers at that instant, for example.

Each check is a row under **Check it yourself**: matches, differs, or can't check. Nothing in the record check comes from Mitoshi's servers.

## What it does not prove

A proof shows that a recorded print is the signed price the verifier accepts. It does not say the house quoted fairly, or that a position was yours: those are on the [record](/explore/record) and in your own receipts.
