<AppLink href="/earn">Earn</AppLink> has four tabs — **Maker**, **Range**, **Parlay**, **Boost** — and its head names what each earns: "Earn the spread". Each is a contract you supply pathUSD to for shares; each says what it holds against and how a supplier loses money. None shows an APY.

| Tab | Contract | Earns the | Where the money goes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maker | MarketMakerVault | **spread** | It quotes both sides of the crypto Windows with its suppliers' cash and earns the gap between them. |
| Range | RangeReserve | **margin** | It is the counterparty to every Range and Moonshot round and locks the payout until the closing print. |
| Parlay | ParlayReserve | **edge** | It keeps the stake when a ticket misses a leg and pays in full when one hits them all. |
| Boost | LeverageReserve | **premium** | It fronts the rest of a boosted position and takes a premium up front. |

## Supply and withdraw

The action card sits beside the reserve's card, inside the first screen; on a phone a **Supply · Withdraw** dock follows you down.

- **Supply** is one passkey confirmation from your account, never fee-sponsored. Shares start at 1.0000; the card shows the share price against it.
- **Withdraw** takes any part of what is idle; **Max** takes all of it. What is deployed on live Windows, or locked behind live tickets and boosts, comes back as they settle.
- **Your position:** worth now, what you put in, **realized** — money paid back above cost — and **on paper** at today's share price.
- An admin may pause **new** supply. Settlement, merges and withdrawals keep running.

## Where the capital is

On **Maker**, a bar splits the vault's capital by asset, deployed and idle, over its Windows — **Live** and **Settled** — each with its inventory, its book, and **Merge** or **Settle** when one is due. On the reserves, a bar shows what is free and what is locked or fronted, with the slots in use and the bounds below.

## What bounds the risk

The bounds are the deployed contracts' own parameters, and the tab reads them live. At deployment:

| Contract | A few of its bounds |
| --- | --- |
| Maker vault | At most 60 % of capital deployed, $200 on one Window, 8 Windows at once, quotes between 5¢ and 95¢ with at least a 2¢ spread, none in a Window's last 45 s |
| RangeReserve | A 12 % margin, payouts up to $500, its own volatility for BTC, ETH and SOL and the fair-value guard's for every other Series |
| ParlayReserve | Up to 3 legs, payouts up to $500, legs within 5¢ of the fair-value model |
| LeverageReserve | Up to 3×, a premium of 8 % of what it fronts, at most $200 fronted per position |

## Who quotes the vault

The ops **earn-quoter** holds the vault's maker role. It quotes the live crypto Windows — each Series' newest two, 5, 15 and 60 minutes — on the fair-value guard's model at the signed spot, at most 8 at once, and settles closed Windows. With no maker set, the tab says **Maker off**. Range, Moonshot and Parlay price against the depth the books hold.

<Callout title="Seen live">
On 29 September a supplier put $50 into the vault on Moderato at 1.000 a share. The vault quoted a five-minute Window, both sides were hit and merged at par by a taker, the Window was settled by anyone, and the supplier withdrew half the shares at 1.0024 ([evidence](/help/evidence)).
</Callout>
