Both are tickets against **RangeReserve**, a house reserve. Each is priced from where the Window's book puts the market now and how much the asset moves in the time left, and settles on the same signed closing print as UP and DOWN.

## Pick a Window

Open <AppLink href="/games/range">Range</AppLink> or <AppLink href="/games/moonshot">Moonshot</AppLink>, or the **Range** mode under the terminal's chart. The cadence tabs read **5m · 15m · 1h · Gap**, shortest first, showing only those with a priced Window live. Under them, every live Window of that cadence — BTC, ETH, SOL and the stocks, 24/7 lanes marked — with its time left. A Gap more than two days out is a greyed chip saying when it opens to bands. The page reopens on the lane you last picked.

## Range

Call a band the close lands **inside** — or outside. Start from **Tight**, **Balanced** or **Wide**, nudge the band, then set the stake or the payout. A narrower band pays a bigger multiple. **Hold to place** sends it; the full payout is locked when the round opens, so it can never come up short.

## Moonshot

Aim **Long** or **Short** and pick a reach: ×2, ×3, ×5, ×10 or ×25. The reserve solves the level the print must finish past — long wins at or above it, short at or below. **Fire** places it, the payout locked up front.

## How it is priced

The band's centre is the Window's book: at least 5 contracts resting a side for 20 seconds. The volatility is the reserve's own for BTC, ETH and SOL, and the fair-value guard's for every other Series, on that Series' clock. A book too thin, a spread too wide, a Window about to lock or a signed price too far from the book — the ticket names it before any prompt and takes nothing. Each call asks your passkey; one-tap keys don't cover the reserve.
