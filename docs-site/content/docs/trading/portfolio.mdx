## The P&L rule

A position's P&L is **what CLOSE pays now** less the **open cost** of what you hold.

- **What CLOSE pays** is the walk down the book's bids for your whole holding. Once the Window has its answer it is the known payout; while the Window is locked, or nothing bids, the row is valued at its open cost and says why: "Locked · settles", "No bids".
- **A thin book** — bids for only part of the holding — reads "N% sellable": P&L on that part, against its share of the open cost, and the rest at cost. CLOSE sells what fills and the rest keeps its cost.
- **Open cost** is per side. A buy adds what it paid; a sale or a merge takes its share out at the average cost; a mint costs half a dollar a side. So Reduce then re-enter doesn't inflate your cost.
- **Trading Balance** bets use the vault's own tally when it is exact, else its booked fills replayed at average cost.
- **A Boost's** cost is your stake less what it has paid back; it pays (held − fronted) ÷ stake.
- **Totals** — equity, the P&L card's return — run over priced rows only, and say how many are unpriced.
- **TP / SL** judge the whole holding at that same walk, in the tab and in the exit keeper, so a thin book isn't read as a drawdown.

On 12 October the rule was checked against the chain on Moderato, to the base unit: a wallet that bought, Reduced and re-entered; a Trading Balance that tapped twice, closed part and re-entered; and a thin book, where a wallet holding and a 2× Boost could sell only part, the Boost closed part then the rest, and the wallet closed what filled ([evidence](/help/evidence)). It ships with the next web release.

## Portfolio

<AppLink href="/portfolio">Portfolio</AppLink> reads your money and your bets from the chain.

- **Your money:** what you can bet with now — your account and your Trading Balance — with what is yours but not spendable here listed apart: cash behind resting orders, and engine credit on settled Windows that **Collect** pays out. **Add money** claims test dollars; **Withdraw** sends pathUSD out.
- **Your bets — Open:** one row per held side, wallet and Trading Balance together, Boosts on their own cards; staked, worth now, P&L. **Cash out** sells; a locked Window pays at settlement.
- **Your bets — History:** one account history — the wallet's rounds, the Trading Balance's and Boost's — as WIN, LOSS, VOID or CLOSED, with net P&L = what came back − what went in, and a realised P&L curve over 7 days, 30 days or all time.

## Trader Edge

<AppLink href="/portfolio/edge">Trader Edge</AppLink> turns your settled Windows into a record: net result and return, win rate, profit factor, average Window, deepest slide, when in the day you do best, your payoff shape, your tier and badges, and a CSV of every settled Window at full precision. It is calculated in your browser from your fills and the settlement rule.
