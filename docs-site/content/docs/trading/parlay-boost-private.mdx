## Parlay

Two or three legs on one ticket at <AppLink href="/parlay">Parlay</AppLink>, or from the terminal's **Parlay** mode, where UP and DOWN add the Window to the slip. The page lists every live Window by cadence, with each asset's close streak; the slip rides above the dock until you open it and **Hold to place**. The chances multiply down, so the payout multiplies up — and it is all or nothing: one miss and the stake is lost.

- **ParlayReserve** prices each leg off the Window's book — refusing a leg priced more than 5¢ under the fair-value guard's model — with a floor for legs that settle at the same instant.
- The winning payout is locked before the ticket is accepted. A book too thin for it is refused by name, before any prompt.
- Legs resolve in close order, by anyone. Each ticket asks your passkey.
- Legs need the fair-value guard's model, which covers every series, crypto and stocks.

## Boost

A leveraged UP or DOWN from the terminal's **Boost** mode or chips. **LeverageReserve** fronts the rest of the position for a premium it keeps, and is repaid first from a sale.

- **Your loss is capped at your stake.** A boost has a knock-out line and can be knocked out before the Window closes; Mitoshi's leverage keeper settles and knocks out positions, and both actions pay the owner.
- It refuses to open a boost that would start under its knock-out line, one priced above the market model, or one a void would leave the reserve short on.
- Boost opens on any Window the guard prices, stocks included. **Hold to boost** asks your passkey; it closes from its own row.
- **Short** in More opens the terminal on Boost with DOWN picked.

## Private

The terminal's **Private** mode, or **Private** on a Markets ticket, bets from a private balance on **PrivateDesk**. Kept separate from your wallet, so it is harder to link back to you — **not anonymous**.

- You deposit to the desk and set an allowance it may spend. One passkey prompt signs the bet's text; it moves nothing by itself.
- The desk then makes three transactions that never name you and the Window together: charge your allowance into a pool, fund a throwaway slot, buy in that slot. Payouts come home the same way, and only you can withdraw.
- Always 1×, a stake of $1 to $25. The claim is kept with your account; cash it out from Portfolio's **Private** pocket.
- What it is not: anonymity. A charge and a slot's funding land seconds apart for the same figure, and the desk process sees both halves.

## Seen live

Through the browser on Moderato, sponsored by the relay: a two-leg parlay in one prompt and a 2× boost in one prompt, with refusals named before signing (`ThinBook`, `BelowFairValue`, `LegOutOfOrder`). The private desk is deployed and passed its tests on a Moderato fork; a private bet through the browser is not recorded yet. Receipts are in [evidence](/help/evidence).
