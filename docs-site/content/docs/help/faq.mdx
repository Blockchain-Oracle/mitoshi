### Is this real money?

No. Mitoshi runs on Tempo testnet. Its dollars are testnet pathUSD from the faucet, with no value. The order books, prints, settlement and receipts are real contracts on a real chain.

### What decides who wins?

Two signed RedStone prints, checked on chain: the Window's open and its close. Close at or above the open pays UP; below pays DOWN. The live line on the chart never decides anything. [More](/trading/settlement).

### Can Mitoshi take my money or change a result?

No contract lets it. Your money moves by your signature or a key or grant you made, inside its bounds on chain; results come from signed prints and the engine's rule, with no admin void. Mitoshi's servers can stall — then no new Windows open and payouts wait — but anyone can still settle and collect, and you can withdraw. [Who can move what](/safety/who-can-do-what), [if Mitoshi stops](/safety/if-mitoshi-stops).

### What can a one-tap key do if my device is stolen?

Buy on the engine with up to $25 of pathUSD a day, and sell, cancel or redeem what your account holds there, until it expires within 24 hours. It can't transfer, withdraw or call anything else, and it can't be copied out of your browser. Revoke it from Account & keys. [The worst case](/safety/worst-case).

### Why did my call not fill?

A tap takes what rests on the book, immediate-or-cancel. If the price moved, the terminal asks again at the new price; if the book is empty, nothing fills and nothing is taken. Between Windows, or in a halt, UP and DOWN are off and say why.

### Why is my Window void?

No reliable print arrived before its deadline — on testnet, most often a RedStone public-gateway window. Both sides are returned at half, $0.50 a contract.

### Do I need to collect winnings?

No. The settler pays winners a few minutes after the close; until then your equity counts the payout. Collecting yourself only hurries it.

### Why can't I trade stock tokens yet?

The contracts are deployed on Moderato, but the Robinhood Chain side waits on testnet ETH and the screens are being built. You can already call nine stocks as Windows. [More](/stocks/overview).

### Is Sensei advice?

No. It reads the Window with you and recommends; you place every call.

### Where is the code?

The repository is private for now. These guides name the files they were checked against, and every receipt here is in the repository's acceptance records.
