Checked against app revision `11d85e0` on 13 October 2026. Mitoshi runs on **Tempo testnet (Moderato)** only; nothing is deployed on Tempo mainnet.

## Live on testnet

- **Trade:** the terminal, Markets and each ticker's hub, Portfolio and Trader Edge, Parlay, Range and Moonshot on every Series, Boost and Short, Private, exits with the app closed.
- **Money:** passkey sign-up with test dollars, one-tap keys, the Trading Balance, Earn's four reserves, Withdraw.
- **Automate:** strategies, copy and fade, the studio with its test read, the agents board and cockpits, the Memory Market over MPP, house agent #3 on a model.
- **Games:** the hall, Beat the coin, Line Rider and Candle Hop on the real prints, Lucky, Duel on the deployed arena, ranks and history.
- **Stocks and tokens:** Themes, Launch.
- **X:** sign in with X, link, fund an X balance, trade from X, X recovery.
- **Records and social:** proofs, Stats, the leaderboard and profiles, the season league, share cards, rooms and takes, Activity, price alerts and notifications, Sensei, Reels, News, Status and the halts board.

No page in the app is a placeholder.

## Limits you will meet

- **Test money.** pathUSD from the faucet, once a day per account, with no value.
- **24/7 stock lanes** pause around 04:00 ET daily; AMD and PLTR have none. Pre-IPO names are listed as paused.
- **Range and Moonshot** need 5 contracts resting a side for 20 seconds; a fresh Window says "Book too thin" until then.
- **Lucky and Duel** deal only 15-minute and one-hour Windows.
- **The public RPC** is shared and rate-limited; a busy minute can slow reads.
- **The private desk** is deployed and fork-tested; no private bet through the browser is recorded yet.
- **Settlement run.** The venue's 24-hour settlement run has not passed: most of its voids came from RedStone's public-gateway windows. A fresh 24 hours spanning a 04:00 ET is still to run.
