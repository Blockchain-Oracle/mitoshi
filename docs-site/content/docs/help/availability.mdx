Checked against app revision `6a6fb7f` on 13 October 2026. Mitoshi runs on **Tempo testnet (Moderato)** only; nothing is deployed on Tempo mainnet.

## Live on testnet

- **Trade:** the terminal, Markets and each ticker's hub, Portfolio and Trader Edge, Parlay, Range and Moonshot, Boost, Private, exits with the app closed.
- **Money:** passkey sign-up with test dollars, one-tap keys, the Trading Balance, Earn's four tabs, Withdraw.
- **Automate:** strategies, copy and fade, the builder, the agents board, the Memory Market over MPP, house agent #3 on a model.
- **Games:** the hall, Practice, Line Rider and Candle Hop with a replay-checked board, Range and Moonshot.
- **Records and social:** proof pages, Stats, the leaderboard and profiles, the season league, share cards, rooms and takes, price alerts and notifications, Sensei, Reels, News, Status and the halts board.

## Limits you will meet

- **Test money.** pathUSD from the faucet, once a day per account, with no value.
- **24/7 stock lanes** pause around 04:00 ET daily; AMD and PLTR have none. NFLX and pre-IPO names are listed as paused.
- **Range and Moonshot** price BTC, ETH and SOL: the Range reserve has a volatility for those three. Parlay, Boost and keeper exits price every series through the fair-value guard.
- **The public RPC** is shared and rate-limited; a busy minute can slow reads.
- **The private desk** is deployed and fork-tested; no private bet through the browser is recorded yet.
- **Settlement run.** The venue's 24-hour settlement run has not passed: most of its voids came from RedStone's public-gateway windows. A fresh 24 hours spanning a 04:00 ET is still to run.
